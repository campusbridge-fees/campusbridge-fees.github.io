How payment works

  1. Choose your fee and request a quote. In WhatsApp, add the institution or provider and your payment deadline.
  2. Confirm the total and payment method. First-time customers pay the full confirmed amount in advance through bank transfer, bKash, Nagad, Rocket, or upay. Returning customers can discuss flexible payment arrangements on WhatsApp.
  3. Use a payment option supported by the provider. Sign in yourself if the provider requires it. Ask us whether the provider offers a guest payment link, an invoice, or an authorized payer option. Keep passwords and one-time codes private.
